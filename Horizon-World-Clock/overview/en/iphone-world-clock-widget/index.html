iPhone widget setup

How to add a world clock widget on iPhone

See the time abroad without opening an app. Each Horizon World Clock widget can show the local time and day or night in four cities you choose.

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Get Horizon World Clock on the App Store. It requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
  2. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen. On iOS 18 or later, tap Edit and then Add Widget. On iOS 17, tap the + button in the top-left corner.
  3. Search for Horizon World Clock, choose Cities for each widget, then tap Add Widget.

Choose which cities to show

  1. Touch and hold the clock widget you added, then tap Edit Widget.
  2. Choose the cities you want in the four slots. Each unfilled slot uses the city in the corresponding slot in the app. Check that all four cities are different.
  3. To show another set of cities, add a second widget and repeat these steps. Choose Same cities as app if you want the widget to mirror all four cities from the app.

Refresh timing and weather

iOS schedules Home Screen widget refreshes. Open the app to see the latest time.

In the weather-enabled iPhone version, you can turn weather effects and temperature on separately. See the weather demo and setup steps.
