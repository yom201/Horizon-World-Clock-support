ScheduleCam

Privacy Policy

November 19, 2026

ScheduleCam is an Android app by ICHITAP that schedules captures and can save them to the Google Drive account you connect. This policy explains how the app handles captures, information from Google, and remote capture requests.

Captures and device storage

When you schedule a capture or enable remote capture, ScheduleCam can use the camera while the device is locked. If you select video with audio, it also uses the microphone. The app stores captures, schedule settings, capture results, and error records on the device. You can delete photos and videos from the Captures screen.

Google Account and Drive

When you sign in with Google, ScheduleCam uses the email address and authentication information provided by Google to identify you, connect to the selected account, and match remote capture requests. The connected account and information about pending uploads are stored on your Android device.

If you authorize Google Drive, ScheduleCam uses file-level access (drive.file) limited to files it works with. The app creates a ScheduleCam folder in your Drive and uploads captures directly from the device to Google Drive. To avoid duplicate uploads, the app looks up the target folder and previously uploaded files. If a connection is interrupted, the app uses its saved copy to resume the transfer.

If you choose Drive only, ScheduleCam uploads new captures and verifies their file size and checksum in Drive before removing the copies from your Android device. The device keeps the files while uploads are pending.

ScheduleCam uses information obtained from Google to authenticate you, save captures to your Drive, match remote capture requests, and send any Gmail notifications you enable. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Gmail notifications

Gmail notifications are planned for a future Google Play release. In versions where you can enable this feature, ScheduleCam uses Gmail's send-only permission to email remote capture results from and to your connected Google Account. It attaches photos that fit and sends links to videos and larger photos after verifying their upload to Drive. The links keep the file's existing Drive access permissions.

The Android device delivers these messages directly to Gmail. The device keeps pending delivery information and a temporary copy of an attached photo to complete the send. You can turn off Gmail notifications in the app and manage messages in Gmail.

Remote capture requests

When you use remote capture, Google Cloud services process your Google Account identifier, a device notification token, device-matching information, and the capture request, its status, and its timestamps. The relay checks that the request belongs to you, notifies the Android device, and returns the capture status to the browser. Captures are stored on the Android device and, if connected, in your Google Drive according to your save destination.

Website analytics

The English ScheduleCam landing page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views by country and measure page load performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. See Cloudflare’s information about Web Analytics.

Website ad measurement

The Japanese ScheduleCam landing page uses a Google Ads tag when you allow ad measurement. Google may use cookies and ad-click information to measure whether an ad visit leads to a click on a Google Play link. The tag is configured with ad personalization turned off. Change your choice under Ad measurement settings at the bottom of the Japanese landing page. See how Google uses advertising data.

Who receives Google user data

Google receives account information when you sign in. The Android app sends captures and their file metadata directly to your authorized Google Drive. If you enable Gmail notifications in a version that offers them, the app sends the message, attached photo or Drive link directly to Google Gmail. The sender and recipient are the same connected account.

ICHITAP uses Google Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to operate remote capture. The relay verifies your Google sign-in and processes your Google Account identifier, Android device registration, capture request, and result status. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers the request to the registered camera phone. The relay does not receive captures or Google Drive or Gmail access tokens.

ScheduleCam uses these transfers for the features you enable. ICHITAP does not sell Google user data, disclose it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties.

How we protect Google user data

The app connects to Google APIs and the remote capture relay over encrypted HTTPS. Google Drive and Gmail access tokens are obtained through Google Play services when needed and are not stored by ScheduleCam. The app stores its local account settings, pending uploads and captures in private Android app storage. Android backup is disabled for the app.

Cloud Firestore encrypts relay records at rest. The relay verifies Google ID tokens and checks the account that owns each capture request before returning its status. It verifies the registered camera phone before accepting result updates. Browser and Android clients cannot read or write the relay's Firestore records directly.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can stop automatic uploads from the Google Drive setting in the app. You can also revoke access in your Google Account’s connected app settings. To delete uploaded files, delete them in Google Drive. To request deletion of data stored by the remote capture relay, contact support below.
